**Description**

ApexUninstaller helps you understand what an app leaves behind before you remove it.

Choose an app, inspect its related files, and decide exactly what moves to Trash. Every result is grouped and labelled so you can review it with confidence.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Find related caches, preferences, logs, containers, and support files
• Review paths, file sizes, and safety labels before removing anything
• Move selected items to Trash instead of deleting them permanently
• Find orphaned leftovers, duplicate files, and storage-heavy items
• Review user caches, logs, and Xcode Derived Data in System Junk
• See app usage and storage insights to make better cleanup decisions
• Keep an optional, encrypted local Clipboard History for copied text, links, and images up to 8 MB

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your cleanup work stays on your Mac. Clipboard History is off by default, encrypted locally, retained for up to 24 hours, and can be cleared at any time. ApexUninstaller never uploads clipboard content or automatically pastes into another app.

SAFE, CLEAR WORKFLOW

ApexUninstaller asks you to choose your Library folder using the standard macOS picker. You remain in control of the folders and items it can access.

**What’s New in Version 1.0.2**

• New optional Clipboard History for copied text, links, and images up to 8 MB
• Choose a global shortcut, browse image thumbnails, and paste a selected item with Command-V
• Improved utility menu and Clipboard panel behaviour
• Clearer local-data and privacy controls

**App Review Notes**

Clipboard History is opt-in and is disabled by default. It observes pasteboard changes, not typing or keystrokes. The global shortcut uses the system hotkey API; each selectable shortcut includes Command or Control. Text, links, and images up to 8 MB are encrypted locally with a key in the user’s Keychain, retained for at most 24 hours, and are never transmitted. The feature does not request Accessibility or Input Monitoring and never simulates paste or injects input into another app. It excludes known password-manager apps.

To test the core app: choose `~/Library` when the app asks for folder access, select any listed application, inspect its files, and choose the selected items to move to Trash. To test Clipboard History: enable it in Settings, copy text or an image up to 8 MB from a non-password-manager app, press the selected global shortcut, choose an entry, then press Command-V in the original app.
