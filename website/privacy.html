Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

ApexUninstaller ("the App") is a macOS utility that helps users remove applications and selected associated leftover files. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Data Collection

We do NOT collect any personal data. The App operates entirely offline on your device.

  • No analytics: We do not track usage patterns or behavior.
  • No network requests: The App does not connect to the internet.
  • No account required: No sign-up, login, or registration needed.
  • No cloud storage: All data stays on your Mac.

3. File System Access

The Mac App Store edition asks you to select ~/Library with the standard folder picker and stores a security-scoped bookmark locally. The Direct edition runs without App Sandbox and can scan ~/Library directly. Full Disk Access is optional, is never enabled automatically, and must be granted by you in System Settings.

  • Additional scan folders are always selected explicitly by you.
  • Bookmarks or selected folder paths are stored on your device only.
  • Full Disk Access can be revoked at any time in System Settings.
  • Used solely to find applications, leftovers, and selected user-level junk files you choose to review or clean.

4. Notifications

The App may send local notifications to remind you about cleanup opportunities. These notifications:

  • Are generated locally on your device.
  • Do not send any data to external servers.
  • Can be disabled in the App's Settings.

5. Menu Bar Feature

If enabled, the App displays an icon in your menu bar for quick access. This feature:

  • Runs entirely on your device.
  • Does not collect any additional data.
  • Can be disabled in the App's Settings.

6. Data Storage

The App stores only:

  • Your language preference (in UserDefaults).
  • Security-scoped bookmarks or selected scan-folder paths, depending on the edition (in UserDefaults).
  • Cleanup history and ignored app identifiers (locally in UserDefaults).
  • Feature settings like menu bar, scheduled scan, and notification preferences (in UserDefaults).

Text reports are written only to locations you choose with the macOS save dialog. No personal information, file contents, or browsing data is ever collected or transmitted.

7. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

8. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
