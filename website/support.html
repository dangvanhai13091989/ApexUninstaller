Frequently Asked Questions

How do I grant Library access?

The Direct edition scans ~/Library without a folder picker. The Mac App Store edition asks you to select ~/Library once. Full Disk Access is optional and can be opened from Direct edition Settings.

Is it safe to delete the files?

Uninstall, Reset, and System Junk cleanup move selected items to Trash when macOS permits it. You can review detailed paths before cleaning.

Will it delete important system files?

The app does not target protected macOS system folders. It may show user-level Apple app caches such as Safari or QuickLook, and lets you review detailed paths before cleaning.

What is Reset Mode?

Reset Mode moves selected app data, preferences, caches, and containers to Trash while keeping the app itself installed.

What are Safe, Likely, and Review labels?

They explain how confidently ApexUninstaller matched each leftover. Review items are not selected by default.

How do I enable Menu Bar icon?

Open Settings (from toolbar) and toggle "Show in Menu Bar" on. The icon will appear in your menu bar for quick access.

Where are reports saved?

Reports are saved only to the location you choose in the macOS save dialog.

What is the Dashboard?

The Dashboard shows disk space analysis: total junk size, top apps with the most leftover files, and a category breakdown. Open it from the toolbar.

Can I drag & drop apps?

Yes! Drag any .app file from Finder onto the ApexUninstaller window to instantly scan it for leftovers and uninstall.

How do I add custom scan locations?

Click the folder icon in the toolbar to add folders like /Users/Shared, Steam libraries, or external drives where you have apps installed.

Can I hide apps from the list?

Yes, use the Ignore button on any app's detail view. Ignored apps won't appear in the list. You can restore them from the toolbar.

Which macOS versions are supported?

macOS 14.0 (Sonoma) or later.

How do I change the language?

Click the flag icon in the toolbar to switch between 8 supported languages.
